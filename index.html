Terms & Conditions

Loan Eligibility

To be eligible for a loan with 9 Loans, applicants must:

  • Be at least 18 years of age
  • Have a valid ID document
  • Have a regular source of income
  • Have an active bank account

Collateral Requirements

At 9 Loans, we require collateral for all loan applications. The collateral value must be at least twice the amount of the loan requested. Acceptable items for collateral include:

  • Electronic devices (laptops, smartphones, tablets)
  • Jewelry and precious metals
  • Vehicles (cars, motorcycles)
  • Other valuable assets subject to evaluation

Repayment Terms

Loan repayment terms are strictly enforced. Late payments may result in additional fees and penalties. Interest rates are fixed as stated in your loan agreement and are calculated based on the loan duration selected.

Privacy Policy

9 Loans respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. We collect only necessary information required for loan processing and never share your data with third parties without your explicit consent.
